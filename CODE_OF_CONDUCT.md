# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome evidence-based, respectful collaboration across different tools, organizations, and levels of experience.

- Discuss files, commands, risks, and reproducible behavior rather than attacking people.
- Protect customer data, private repositories, credentials, and audit evidence.
- Clearly separate verified facts from inference and recommendations.
- Do not submit harassment, discriminatory content, malicious automation, or fabricated evidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict accounts that repeatedly disrupt collaboration. Security concerns should follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); platform abuse should also be reported through GitHub.
